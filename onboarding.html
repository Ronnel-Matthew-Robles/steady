Steady is on

Reduce motion and calm the web

Steady is now on for every site. It quietly calms animations, pauses autoplaying video and audio, and freezes animated GIFs, while leaving pages fully scrollable, clickable, and typeable.

Need motion back on a particular site? Open that site, click the Steady icon in your toolbar, and turn on Allow motion on this site.

See it working right now

  • Scroll-reveal demo: content appears instantly instead of sliding in as you scroll.
  • CSS animation gallery: click any effect and it snaps to its finished state, no motion.
  • Parallax demo: the background scrolls with the page instead of floating.
  • Animated GIFs on Wikipedia: every GIF holds still on its first frame.
