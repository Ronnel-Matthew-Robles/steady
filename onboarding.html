Your controls

Two switches and one badge

Steady everywhere

The master switch, in the toolbar popup and above. On by default, on every site.

Allow motion on this site

Want a particular site animated? Open it, click the Steady icon, flip this on. Steady remembers your choice for that site only.

The off badge

A clean toolbar icon means Steady is calming the page. A small badge appears only when it is not, either because you allowed this site or turned Steady off.

Quiet by design. No servers, no analytics, no account. Steady talks to no one; the only network activity it can ever cause is re-reading an image the page already loaded. Your settings never leave this device.
