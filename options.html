Steady Settings

Settings

Everything is stored on this device and nowhere else.

Master switch

Steady everywhere Calms every site. The popup and Alt+Shift+G flip this too.

What Steady calms

Animations and transitions CSS and Web Animations settle instantly into their finished state. Includes parallax pinning.
Autoplaying video and audio Paused until you press play. Media you start yourself is never touched.
Animated images (GIF, WebP) Held on their first frame instead of looping.
Smooth scrolling Anchor jumps and programmatic scrolling become instant.

Comfort (all off by default)

Soften colors Gently caps page brightness and color saturation for eye-strain relief.
30%
Reduce flashing Lowers the brightness and contrast of video, canvas, and animated images to blunt rapid light and dark swings. This reduces intensity only; it is not a guarantee of safety and must not be relied on to prevent photosensitive reactions.
Panic dim Instantly dims the whole page. Press Alt+Shift+D on any page (press again to undo), or flip this switch. Available even while Steady is off.

Sites where motion is allowed

No exceptions yet. On any site, open the Steady popup and flip "Allow motion on this site" (or press Alt+Shift+S).
